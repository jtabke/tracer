# UX competitive critique — system context, not record-by-record work

**Status:** public-source desk review, 2026-10-10. Design hypotheses, not observed competitor usability, a product ranking, selected architecture or implementation approval.

## 1. What changed in our investigation

The owner reports that Jama feels like one requirement or relationship at a time and does not provide a useful big picture for complex systems with parents, siblings and children (UR-7). The owner also finds our generated concepts insufficiently impactful/realistic, requests competitor critique including Carbon, and explicitly rejects copying legacy interaction patterns (UR-8).

The working problem is **context reconstruction**: how much relevant information must an engineer remember or reopen to understand and act on a connected part of the system? That is a hypothesis to test against actual tasks, not a measured diagnosis of all competitors.

“Big picture” is not necessarily every object drawn at once. It is an understandable relevant whole: system intent, the family being worked on, shared contributions, cross-discipline paths and what lies outside the displayed scope. A graph can still be a one-record browser; a table can preserve excellent system context. Modernity must include useful interaction, readable hierarchy, keyboard access, direct manipulation and recovery—not merely typography, whitespace or animation.

## 2. Evidence and limits

- Reviewed official help, release material, public screenshots, a published animation, an Arena webinar transcript/PDF and Carbon source. Screenshot observations below are visual inspection, **not hands-on task execution**.
- No vendor accounts, paid trials, installations or customer data. No claims about actual click counts, learning time, performance, accessibility conformance or market uniqueness.
- Version coverage varies: Polarion/Teamcenter 2606 release material; Windchill 13.1.2 documentation; currently accessible Jama help; Valispace documentation with 2024 screenshot names; Carbon main snapshot `aa5a8a032e9aac07be7db007f26ee65b6d2e9aae`. Arena's public examples are not a verified current release; its PDF includes historical sample records. These are not a uniform latest-version benchmark.
- Carbon here means [carbon.ms / crbnos/carbon](https://github.com/crbnos/carbon), not IBM's Carbon design system. It is primarily manufacturing ERP/MES/QMS, with engineering/BOM and genealogy workflows; manufacturing traceability is not interchangeable with requirements/evidence traceability.
- Public marketing descriptions are attributed, not treated as proof. For example, Teamcenter's scale/performance claims were not benchmarked. An older Teamcenter 12.2 article surfaced in search and was not used to characterize current UI.

## 3. Product critique

### Carbon — strongest modern interaction reference in this sample, not a template

**Visible:** its [BOM screenshot](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/crbnos/carbon/aa5a8a032e9aac07be7db007f26ee65b6d2e9aae/.github/assets/readme/bom-light.webp) shows several expanded assembly levels, sibling rows, revision/quantity cues, a “Used In” perspective and adjacent planning/supersession details. Its [traceability screenshot](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/crbnos/carbon/aa5a8a032e9aac07be7db007f26ee65b6d2e9aae/.github/assets/readme/traceability-light.webp) shows Graph/Table, a “1 hop” control, selected-node context, produced-by/consumed-by lists and multi-selection guidance. The restrained chrome makes the work prominent.

**Source-backed interaction:** [table source](https://github.com/crbnos/carbon/blob/aa5a8a032e9aac07be7db007f26ee65b6d2e9aae/apps/erp/app/components/Table/Table.tsx) exposes optional inline editing, saved views, row expansion, contextual actions, stable row IDs and range selection. These are configurable capabilities, not proof every screen enables them. [BOM source](https://github.com/crbnos/carbon/blob/aa5a8a032e9aac07be7db007f26ee65b6d2e9aae/apps/erp/app/modules/items/ui/Item/BillOfMaterial.tsx) expands a selected material editor within the list and uses URL parameters for contextual selection; this is more concrete than a marketing claim. [Configurator documentation](https://docs.carbon.ms/docs/reference/configurator) places rules on relevant method fields and groups parameters rather than presenting one giant form.

**Borrow to test:** progressive disclosure within the working surface; explicit neighborhood scope; alternate perspectives on the same selected information; contextual editing and readable hierarchy. Borrow behavior, not source code or its manufacturing ontology.

**Improve rather than copy:** the traceability example still centers one entity and one-hop genealogy. A BOM tree cannot by itself represent a shared requirement contributing to two intentions, cycles or several relationship meanings. Long labels truncate in the shown BOM. Neither image proves multi-item reasoning, reliable proposal comparison or evidence applicability. A modern skin does not resolve those questions.

### Jama Connect — more capable than “no overview”, but context concerns remain valid

**Documented/visible:** [Trace View help](https://help.jamasoftware.com/ah/en/get-started/getting-started/exploring-the-jama-connect-workspace/trace-view.html) describes source items, upstream/downstream levels, configured fields, missing coverage, adding relationships and editing via a slide-out. Its [trace screenshot](https://help.jamasoftware.com/ah/en/image/img-6548b75ed8ecf7c52b7280c45dd69dd2.png) shows source/downstream columns and sibling rows. The [editor screenshot](https://help.jamasoftware.com/ah/en/image/img-5f7b33fdba3a87948777216626aede34.png) shows the detail panel covering much of the downstream area. The help explicitly says shared downstream items can appear multiple times while being counted once. [Relationship documentation](https://help.jamasoftware.com/en/manage-content/coverage-and-traceability/relationships.html) describes upstream changes marking downstream relationships suspect.

**Borrow to test:** configured level fields, visible relationship expectations, direct linking in the trace context, recognition of repeated identity.

**Improve rather than copy:** readable full statements while comparing a family; retain the parent's intent and sibling context while editing; avoid replacing the working surface with a detail form. The owner-reported context loss is meaningful even though Trace View exists. The available screenshots do not establish that users can maintain broad context through a complete task. Suspect links are prompts to reassess, not proof every neighbor must change or evidence is invalid.

### Polarion — document continuity and batch work

**Visible/documented:** [2606 release material](https://blogs.sw.siemens.com/polarion/polarion-alm-2606-whats-new-and-noteworthy/) describes inline table editing. Its [document screenshot](https://blogs.sw.siemens.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/4/2026/05/New_Brand_Colors_and_Polarion_Icon_3_2606.png) shows several full requirements, selected work-item properties and a document outline together; the [table screenshot](https://blogs.sw.siemens.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/4/2026/05/New_Brand_Colors_and_Polarion_Icon_2_2606.png) shows many work items next to selected detail.

**Borrow to test:** authoring/review in a continuous document; optional structured fields around prose; batch-friendly edits without opening a new record for every statement.

**Improve rather than copy:** the visible document sequence is not the complete relationship network. Dense toolbars and simultaneous panels compete for reading width; a selected-item link list still requires reconstruction of a cross-cutting family. Test document continuity without inheriting its whole navigation model.

### Teamcenter — overview exists; scale does not equal comprehension

**Visible/documented:** [2606 release material](https://blogs.sw.siemens.com/teamcenter/teamcenter-2606/) links a [Relations Graph animation](https://blogs.sw.siemens.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/14/2026/06/teamcenter-2606-relationships.gif). The first frame has incoming/outgoing expansion, filters, group/layout, highlight-thread and detail controls. An extracted frame at about eight seconds displays a broad multi-level network with many crossing links and small labels. The release describes cross-domain impact exploration spanning requirements, design, tests, BOM and simulation; those breadth claims are vendor documentation, not our execution evidence.

**Borrow to test:** deliberate expansion of scope, grouping, typed cross-domain context and highlighting one explanatory thread within a larger network.

**Improve rather than copy:** a wall of tiny nodes may show more while explaining less. Users need readable statements, family comparison and useful actions at the chosen scale—not just pan/zoom. Do not treat the ability to display many nodes as validated understanding or import an enterprise governance workflow by default.

### Windchill — explicit collection, but another working set to reconstruct

**Documented/visible:** [13.1.2 change-impact help](https://support.ptc.com/help/windchill/r13.1.2.0/en/Windchill_Help_Center/reqmgmt/RMChangeImpact.html) walks through collecting upstream/downstream local/external objects into a change record. The [collection screenshot](https://support.ptc.com/help/windchill/r13.1.2.0/en/Windchill_Help_Center/images/CollectObjectWindow.png) includes a collection-rule column, so a result has some explanation of why it was included.

**Borrow to test:** distinguish the current selection from the resulting scope; expose inclusion reasons and external-object identity.

**Improve rather than copy:** the visible flow moves through collection windows and affected-object tables. It does not show the parent/sibling/child structure while interpreting a consequence. An explicit list is useful for review but should not force the user to reconstruct the system separately.

### Arena — useful change package, not a system-thinking canvas

**Documented/visible:** the [BOM & Beyond webinar transcript](https://www.arenasolutions.com/resources/plm-webinars/bom-beyond/) demonstrates a field overheating issue, proposed thermal-fuse addition, BOM redlines, approvals and implementation tasks. This is a vendor demo, not evidence of the actual effectiveness of the fix. [ECO examples PDF](https://www.arenasolutions.com/wp-content/uploads/arena-eco-template.pdf), pages 3–4, shows affected-item revision/lifecycle columns and proposed BOM redlines within a change order. The public material includes historical example records, so do not infer current visual styling from it.

**Borrow to test:** a coherent package connecting reason, exact changes, affected information and unfinished actions; distinguish proposal/revision states; comparison at the place where a decision is made.

**Improve rather than copy:** tabs separate items, files, approvals and implementation, and a dense change package can still obscure causal paths. Use the package as review context, not a mandatory bureaucratic starting point for every small authoring task. A BOM delta is not a system-requirement satisfaction argument.

### Valispace / Requirements and Systems Portal — broader context with a readability trade-off

**Documented/visible:** [Connections Graph help](https://docs.valispace.com/vhd/connections-graph-status) describes specification grouping, compact/full display, filtering and relationship types; its [screenshot](https://docs.valispace.com/__attachments/1636073533/image-20240327-144934.png?inst-v=fd4a0fd8-56d5-48dc-b578-82615c205aa4) shows multiple parents/children across specification context but very small text. [Breakdown documentation](https://docs.valispace.com/vhd/requirements-breakdown-process) describes linking existing parents/children directly in table cells and creating children from a parent's detail.

**Borrow to test:** group meaningful families, expose multiple relationship kinds and link existing information directly without making duplicates.

**Improve rather than copy:** compact identifiers and status coloring are not enough to reason about long statements; a crowded legend/filter panel can reduce useful canvas space. Verification coloring should not be imported as a single “safe/complete” truth indicator. These are accessible documentation examples, not a verified current-product run.

## 4. What this means for differentiation

**A graph, matrix, side panel, inline editor or clean visual style is not independently differentiating.** These already appear in the reviewed evidence. A plausible differentiation hypothesis is:

> Work on a meaningful system neighborhood as one coherent, editable context; preserve readable intent, shared identity and explanatory relationships as the user moves between whole, family and detail.

Candidate behaviors to test—not a specification or approved implementation:

| Borrowed insight                  | Tracer improvement hypothesis                                                                                                                   | Task that would expose failure                                                                                             |
| --------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Contextual details and expansion  | Pin essential system intent and family context while opening/editing detail, rather than repeatedly replacing the workspace.                    | Read a parent, compare three siblings, edit one child and return without reopening the parent or losing a draft.           |
| Graph/table/document perspectives | Change representation of the same working set and selection, not start over in a separate page.                                                 | Use readable prose to assess intent, compare fields across the family, then inspect a cross-link with the same identities. |
| Scope and thread controls         | Keep included levels/relationship meanings, omitted branches and result limits legible; expose why an item is included.                         | Explain two paths to a shared child and identify an unmodeled cross-discipline consequence.                                |
| Inline/batch editing              | Author, connect and compare several related items where they are shown; leave low-frequency administrative fields secondary.                    | Add a missing sibling, connect an existing shared test and edit a long statement without jumping among record forms.       |
| Redlines and change packages      | Show exactly what changed alongside unchanged intent and assessment rationale; do not replace the system picture with a generic diff dashboard. | Explain why implementation changed while a system requirement did not, and identify which old evidence inputs need review. |

Do not select a graph-first default from this review. Test an editable family board, readable relationship lanes and document-plus-context as different interaction hypotheses. Meaningful grouping and semantic zoom can help, but can also hide important information or become another forced hierarchy. Each needs a keyboard path, explicit scope and evidence of task fit.

## 5. Why our previous concepts fell short

This is parent critique of our artifacts, not observed user-task evidence:

- The three images mainly changed entry point while retaining list/card/inspector conventions. They did not demonstrate sustained work across parents, siblings and shared children.
- “Software”, “mechanics” and “needs assessment” were category placeholders, not a realistic set of statements, revisions, known relationships and conflicting evidence that would reveal friction.
- A static image cannot expose how context disappears during authoring, linking, changing selection, an interrupted draft or a stale-input review.
- The generated network added unestablished edges; prettier connections did not improve the truthfulness of the scenario.

The owner subsequently requested collaborative visual iteration rather than another coded prototype. Use source-grounded tasks to agree on information hierarchy, layout and interactions in small steps; optional image generation supports visual direction, not product requirements. The current sequence is owned by [ux-plan.md](ux-plan.md), not this historical critique.

## 6. Experiment outcome and current direction

The task-sized fictional family-board recommendation led to the [system-neighborhood experiment](ux-artifacts/system-neighborhood/README.md). Do not treat that recommendation as the next experiment or refine the discarded layout into the product.

Current work compares source-grounded formal and lightweight engineering workflows, their underlying coordination purposes and information needs, followed by small collaborative visual decisions. See [UX plan section 7](ux-plan.md#7-immediate-next-step). Seek sanitized real artifacts and observations alongside explicitly labeled provisional examples; do not require the owner to invent all scenarios. A competitor component or screenshot is supporting inspiration, not a requirement.

This review does not establish representative task evidence, a unique market position, complete accessibility evaluation or authority to rebuild production UI. Owner-reported feedback remains distinct from observed competitor/user behavior.

## 7. Software traceability — adoption hypothesis, not a novel feature claim

The owner asks whether EventSculpt need/code/test tracing adds enough beyond existing development tools to justify adoption. Additional primary-source documentation review, not hands-on comparison:

- [GitHub issue/PR linking](https://docs.github.com/en/issues/tracking-your-work-with-issues/using-issues/linking-a-pull-request-to-an-issue) already connects work intent to changes and supports issue closure on eligible merges. A merged PR/closed issue does not establish every user outcome or deployed state.
- [Sourcegraph Deep Search](https://sourcegraph.com/docs/deep-search) documents natural-language code investigation, navigation, sourced answers, diagrams, cross-repository inventories and migration/cross-reference analysis. Do not claim code-inspection tools cannot analyze impact or produce durable/shareable findings.
- [Codebeamer test-management documentation](https://support.ptc.com/help/codebeamer/r3.2/en/codebeamer/user_guide/95044.html) explicitly integrates tests with requirements, tasks, documentation, source code and the product lifecycle, including runs/results and coverage analysis. Requirement → code → test links and coverage/impact concepts are established ALM capabilities, not Tracer inventions. A separate PTC marketing page was blocked (HTTP 403); no configuration/provider bypass or unseen-page claim.

**Candidate value:** a lower-friction, cross-artifact engineering workspace that preserves intent and approved-versus-realized scope, exposes actionable partial/missing mappings, and relates changed inputs to the recorded evidence/decisions without duplicating their source owners. Complement code search and existing issue/PR/CI workflows; do not become another code browser or require maintaining a parallel specification system. This combination is a usability/adoption hypothesis, not established market uniqueness or proof that competitors cannot do it.

**Current feasibility:** Tracer already models configurable entities/relationships with stable identities/history, external Git/file bindings, local committed-file observations, immutable scoped evaluation receipts, changed/unknown comparisons and review rationale. These provide a foundation for explicitly authored traces and recorded source/evidence drift. Current observation is file-level, not symbol-aware; it is last-observed, not continuous monitoring or semantic correctness analysis. No mature code-linking workflow, rename/symbol resolution, source-host/CI synchronization or automatic implementation conformance is claimed. See [architecture](architecture.md#offline-provenance--implemented-vertical-slice).

**Adoption test proposal:** use one bounded EventSculpt feature in existing Markdown/GitHub/code-search/test workflows as the baseline, then compare a small Tracer walkthrough on the same questions: which approved intent has no/partial implementation trace, which tests address it, and which mappings/evidence need reconsideration after one change? Record useful discoveries, time/context reconstruction, uncertainty/errors and initial plus ongoing link-maintenance cost; no invented numerical target. Include existing-but-unlinked code and a partial implementation. If Tracer supplies no clearer actionable outcome or costs more to maintain than it saves, do not build out a software integration merely to add a graph. The proposal does not authorize implementation or a paid trial.

**Folder follow-up, 2026-10-15 (UR-38):** the owner prefers the category direction and cites Jama's directory-like folders as useful while its sidebar feels limiting. Jama's [Explorer Tree](https://help.jamasoftware.com/ah/en/get-started/getting-started/exploring-the-jama-connect-workspace/explorer-tree.html) documents components, sets, folders and items, with configurable display and movement between the tree and List View. Its [advanced filters](https://help.jamasoftware.com/ah/en/searching-for-content/advanced-filters.html) are saved/shareable rules over fields, tags, relationships, location and other conditions. Its [glossary](https://help.jamasoftware.com/ah/en/reference/glossary.html) distinguishes organizational folders from tags attached to items and relationships between items. These are documented capabilities, not proof of observed usability. The useful Tracer hypothesis is a browsable organizational hierarchy available through several surfaces, with cross-cutting working views and engineering traces kept explicit. The owner's preference does not select Jama's containment rules, copying/reuse model or permanent sidebar composition.

Cytoscape and Gephi identify themselves as open source; Hubble UI's source is public with its project license. Kumu was also examined for [focus by degree/direction](https://docs.kumu.io/guides/focus) and [separate filter/showcase behavior](https://docs.kumu.io/getting-started/first-steps); it is an additional product reference, not included as an open-source application. Its field clustering creates display elements and links; those must not be confused with recorded Tracer engineering relationships.

The older [Cytoscape expand-collapse extension](https://github.com/iVis-at-Bilkent/cytoscape.js-expand-collapse) explicitly says it is no longer maintained and points toward the unified framework. The successor README also contains release/installation caveats; no package readiness or compatibility assessment was performed. These sources justify exploring interactions, not switching Tracer from the current React Flow/ELK study. Project performance claims remain unverified.

**Synthesis:** aggregation and exact inspection should be reversible. A folder/category helps choose meaningful groups; a mixed-detail graph preserves their external relationships; a selected bundle opens the contributing links; a table or matrix exposes dense exact connections. Cosmetic edge bundling alone does not provide counted aggregation, record identity or complete inspection. Automatic community detection may suggest exploratory groups but must not silently assign engineering ownership or change folder membership. The [UX plan](ux-plan.md#scale-interaction-to-explore--independent-detail-for-groups-and-connections) owns the proposed next study and validation questions.

**Blender/n8n follow-up (UR-40), 2026-10-15:** the owner suggests these node editors while questioning n8n at scale. Blender's [node groups](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/5.1/interface/controls/nodes/groups.html) document encapsulated nodes with exposed inputs/outputs, entering/leaving a group and hierarchy breadcrumbs. Its [node editor manual](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/5.0/interface/controls/nodes/node_editors.html) documents Frame Selected/All, context pinning, breadcrumbs and reroute points. Official indexed source text was available; several direct Blender page fetches returned errors, so no live editor or screenshot inspection is claimed. These are versioned interaction references, not a latest-release assessment.

n8n's [Canvas Groups](https://docs.n8n.io/build/understand-workflows/workflow-components/canvas-groups) document named collapsible sections, group descriptions, shared saved grouping and personal browser-local collapse state. Its current rules require a connected chain, prohibit existing grouped nodes and allow external connections only at a group's first/last node. Its [sub-workflow conversion](https://docs.n8n.io/build/flow-logic/convert-to-sub-workflows) creates reusable execution units with explicit constraints; it is distinct from readability-only grouping. No measured scale/usability conclusion follows from these features. Tracer can investigate direct canvas grouping, separate expansion versus entry, and relationship-kind boundary summaries while preserving arbitrary cross-group links and original identity. Do not impose executable input/output boundaries, auto-rewrite engineering traces or require moving records into separate models merely for readability.
